User Guide

How to use the report, read the figures,
and understand each chart and table
Dashboard: mbtraininguk.github.io/Trainingdashboard  ·   Data source: Technical Training Schedule 2026 workbook  ·   Guide revised: November 2026

1. Overview

The Training Dashboard reads the Technical Training Schedule Excel workbook and turns it into interactive charts and tables. It runs entirely in your browser. There's no server and nothing to install, and all the figures are worked out live from the file you load.

It has three tabs:

  • Dashboard: trainer days against target, and how trainers' time is split between training, other activities and unassigned days
  • Room Utilisation: how well the training rooms are booked against the 66% target
  • Course Fill: how full PC and Van courses are against the 85% target, from a separate Course Fill report
What you need Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and the current copy of the Technical Training Schedule 2026 workbook (.xlsx). For the Course Fill tab you also need one or more Course Fill / Performance Report files.

Sheets used from the workbook

Sheet nameWhat it provides
Dashboard (2026) (required)Monthly trainer days, each trainer's cumulative target, the YTD total, and the full-year Other Activities and Unallocated figures
Room Utilisation (2026) (required)Booked and unallocated days per room per month. Drives the room KPIs, charts and tables
Trainer Utilisation (2026)Monthly activity and unallocated days per trainer (used for the YTD view of Other Activities), and annual leave (shown as Holiday)
Schedule 2026The day-by-day schedule. Used for the weekly room heatmap, unique courses per trainer, Trainer Duties and Second Trainer days, and TW5 bookings
Sheet names must match exactly If Dashboard (2026) or Room Utilisation (2026) is missing or renamed, the dashboard won't load and shows a message. If one of the other two is missing, the dashboard still loads, but the parts that depend on it will be empty or less detailed.

2. Loading the data

  1. Open https://mbtraininguk.github.io/Trainingdashboard/ in your browser.
  2. You'll see a drop zone. Drag and drop the workbook onto it, or click it to browse for the file.
  3. The dashboard builds automatically, usually in a second or two.
  4. To load a different file, click "Load different file" at the top right.

The User Manual button at the top of the dashboard opens this guide.

Tip: keeping the figures up to date The dashboard only reads the file you drop in. To see the latest figures, save the workbook, click "Load different file" and drop it in again.

YTD and Full Year

The Dashboard and Room Utilisation tabs each have a toggle at the top. It switches that tab's KPIs, charts and tables between:

  • YTD (Jan – end of the current month): January up to and including the current month. This is the default when the file loads
  • Full Year: all 12 months, including planned future months

KPIHow it is calculated
Team Training DaysSum of all trainers' days in the selected period, taken from the Dashboard sheet. The sub-lines show the number of trainers, the average per trainer, and the total as a % of the team target (green at 100% or more, red below).
Training %Training days ÷ total days × 100. Total days = training days + other activity days + unassigned days.
Other Activities %All non-training activity days (see 3.6) ÷ total days × 100.
Unassigned %Days marked Unallocated ÷ total days × 100.
Overall Utilised %Training % + Other Activities %. This is the headline productivity figure.

3.2 Trainer Overview cards

One card per trainer. Each shows the trainer's YTD days and full-year days, a progress bar against their YTD target, how many days ahead or behind target they are, and the number of unique courses they deliver in the Schedule sheet. The bar and figure are colour-coded by % of YTD target:

80% or more 50–79% Below 50%

Each trainer's target comes from the Cum' Training Days Target column on the Dashboard sheet. If a trainer has no target on the Dashboard sheet, the card says so. The cards always show YTD, whatever the toggle is set to.

3.3 Trainer Days — Monthly chart (bar)

A grouped bar chart with one bar per trainer per month, labelled with the number of days. A dashed yellow line marks the 12.5 days per month guide target.

3.4 Cumulative YTD vs Target chart (line)

The running total for the whole team across the year, with three lines:

  • Team Target (dashed yellow): the cumulative team target from the Dashboard sheet
  • Total Team Delivered (blue): the cumulative days delivered. Each point is labelled with the gap to target, for example +6d in green or −4d in red
  • Projected Trend (dashed purple): carries the average monthly rate so far forward to the end of the period

3.5 Monthly Trainer Summary table

One row per month, with a column for the team target, a column per trainer, and the team total. A coloured dot shows how each trainer did against their own target for that month:

100% or more 70–99% 50–69% Below 50% No target set

A dash (—) means no days that month. In Full Year view, a YTD TOTAL row is added at the bottom.

3.6 Other Activities chart and table

A horizontal bar chart showing the total days spent on each non-training activity, with each one's share of all days (training + activities + unassigned). The table below breaks this down per trainer.

Trainers who joined or left during the year Trainers are only counted for the months they were in post. For example, Victor is counted up to 8 July 2026 and James from 14 September 2026. These dates are set in the dashboard itself, so any new starters or leavers need adding there.

4. Room Utilisation Tab

This tab covers the training rooms TW1+Studio, TW2, TW3, TW5, TW10, TW11 and TR1. The YTD / Full Year toggle updates the KPIs, charts and tables. The heatmap always shows the full year.

TW5 isn't in the workbook's room summaries, so the dashboard counts its bookings from the Schedule 2026 sheet. It only appears if it has bookings.

A grouped bar chart showing each room's utilisation % month by month, with a dashed line at the 66% target. Months with no bookings in any room are left out. It's useful for spotting seasonal patterns or rooms that are always under- or over-used.

4.6 Monthly Booking Detail table

One row per room, with a pair of columns for each month: Booked and Avail (unallocated) days. The booked figure uses the same colours as the Room Detail table.

5. Course Fill Tab

This tab uses a separate file: the Course Fill / Performance Report (.xlsx). Drop one or more files onto the tab's own drop zone. If you drop several years at once, each year gets its own section so you can compare them. The newest year is shown first.

What's counted

  • Rows where Area starts with "Technical" and includes PC or Van
  • Classes with a Class Status of Completed or Released
  • Fill % = students (Stud Ct) ÷ maximum places (Max Ct) × 100, grouped by course start month

The file can have one sheet per year (named "2025", "2026" and so on) or a single sheet. A single sheet is split by year using the start dates. The sheet must have a header row containing Template Code.

5.1 Fill KPIs

Four boxes for the newest year: PC Fill Rate, VN Fill Rate, PC + VN Combined (each with seats filled and the difference from target) and Target (Calex KPI), which shows the 85% target and the previous years' PC and VN rates.

5.2 Chart and table per year

For each year, a bar chart shows PC, VN and combined fill % by month, with a dashed line at 85%. The table underneath gives the same figures with a TOTAL column. Colours:

85% or more 75–84% Below 75%

8. Security & Data Privacy

Your data never leaves your device The dashboard page is served from GitHub Pages, but the files you load are never uploaded. Reading the file, the calculations and the charts all happen inside your browser on your own computer.

How the file is read

When you drop a workbook onto the dashboard, the browser reads it locally in memory using a JavaScript library (SheetJS). The spreadsheet data isn't sent or stored anywhere outside your browser tab. Closing or refreshing the tab clears it straight away.

Internet connection

You need an internet connection to open the page, which also loads the Chart.js and SheetJS libraries from a CDN. After that, loading a file doesn't send anything over the internet.

No cookies, no tracking, no login

The dashboard doesn't set cookies, doesn't save anything in your browser and has no analytics or tracking. It doesn't need a login.

Sharing results

The dashboard page holds no data, so the link can be shared freely. It's just a viewer. To share results, use your browser's Print → Save as PDF to make a snapshot of what's on screen, then send it through your normal secure channels (email, SharePoint and so on).

Sensitive data

The Technical Training Schedule workbook contains employee scheduling information. Store and handle it in line with your organisation's data handling policy, and don't leave it anywhere publicly accessible.

9. Common Questions

Why are some months blank or low in the charts?

In Full Year view, future months only show what has been planned so far. They fill in as the year goes on and the workbook is updated.

What period does YTD cover?

January up to and including the current month, based on today's date. For example, on 17 November 2026, YTD covers January to the end of November 2026, so the current month is included even though it isn't finished.

Why don't the Second Trainer days add to the totals?

Second Trainer days are already counted as trainer days on the Dashboard sheet. They're shown in Other Activities for information only, so they aren't counted twice.

I get "Could not find Dashboard (2026) or Room Utilisation (2026) sheets"

You've loaded the wrong file, or those sheets have been renamed. Check that you're using the current Technical Training Schedule workbook and that the sheet names match those in section 1 exactly.

The heatmap says "No Schedule 2026 sheet found"

The workbook doesn't have a sheet called Schedule 2026, or it has been renamed. The rest of the dashboard still works, but the heatmap, unique course counts, Trainer Duties, Second Trainer and TW5 need that sheet.

How do I update the data?

Update and save the workbook as normal, then click "Load different file" and drop it in again. The whole dashboard rebuilds from scratch.

The page looks fine but no charts appear. What's wrong?

Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Internet Explorer and older browsers aren't supported. Also check you have an internet connection, because the chart library loads when the page opens.

Can I print the dashboard?

Yes. Use File → Print (or Ctrl + P / ⌘ P) and choose "Save as PDF". Landscape with minimum margins gives the best result.
